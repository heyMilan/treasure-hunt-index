Run of show

  1. Alternate teams for each round: Red R1 → Blue R1 → Red R2 → Blue R2, and so on.
  2. For quiz rounds 1, 2, 3 and 5, ask 8 questions aloud. Pass mark: 5/8.
  3. Each team gets one lifeline per quiz round: host may eliminate two options or give a hint. Decide before asking the question.
  4. If below 5/8, offer the rescue task. On completion, hand over the next QR card; award up to 3 rescue points.
  5. Round 4 is a live performance; award up to 5 points: creativity 2, teamwork 2, commitment 1.
  6. Players follow a host-given clue to find a hidden QR checkpoint, scan it, then find you for the host-led round.
  7. After the round is passed—or the rescue task is completed—give the team the next location clue from Clue Planner. Do not give the next clue before they earn it.

Scoreboard

Record total quiz points (max 32 across four quiz rounds) and performance/rescue bonus points.
