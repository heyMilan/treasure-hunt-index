Host setup checklist

  1. Choose 10 safe, distinct hiding places—five for each team.
  2. Print the QR cards and label the back with team and round (keep the QR visible on the front).
  3. Enter a clue below for the location of each QR. Keep clues vague enough to be fun but solvable.
  4. Place the QR cards before guests arrive. Give only the Round 1 clue at the start.
  5. After a team completes a round, give them the clue for their next checkpoint.

For Round 4, a performance challenge replaces the 8-question quiz. After the task, give the clue for Round 5.

Host flow at a glance

Clue → search villa → scan QR → find host → play round → record score → pass / rescue → receive next clue.

Keep the answer key on the Host Desk. Players should never need to use the host device.
